The user supplied the source file rebar_cap_8mm.stl. This package derives its
spring section from that file. Modifications made on 2026-10-16: independent
25–40 mm head diameter, rebuilt 2–8 mm closed base, flat printing face with
1 mm bevel, nominal 6/8/10/12 mm radial variants, a hollow internal guide
(nominal bar + 2 mm bore, 2 mm wall, 13 mm height), and a smooth external
spring-waist reinforcement of up to 0.6 mm radially. Revision 3, dated
2026-10-17, narrows the spring slots to approximately half their prior width,
revises the upper reinforcement taper and refines the mesh sampling.
Revision 4, dated 2026-10-17, replaces the outer neck with a C2 surface of
revolution, retaining the nominal inner grip, guide and narrowed slot outlines.
Revision 5, dated 2026-10-17, enlarges the head bevel to 3 × 3 mm at 45°,
with an adjusted outer shoulder for bases thinner than 3 mm.
