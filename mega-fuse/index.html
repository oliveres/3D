DIN rail MEGA fuse holder

27 mm width · reduced depth · two M8 bolts at 50.8 mm

v4 · longer rail hooks

A separate holder for MEGA fuses, including Victron Energy MEGA fuses with the illustrated footprint. The two terminals stay separate; the fuse connects them. A removable carrier gives access to both bolt heads before assembly.

27 mm width · 1.5 DIN modules · M8 only. Join the two parts with 2 × M4 × 8 socket-head screws and 2 plain M4 nuts.

Default stop spacing: 35.75 mm. Positive widens the gap; negative narrows it. The M8 pitch stays 50.8 mm.

OpenSCAD source · Assembly & printing guide · Dimensioned profile

DIN clip v4: both retaining lips extend 0.50 mm towards the centre. Default retaining-tip gap: 31.80 mm. The rail stop spacing and flange grooves stay the same. The accepted fuse carrier remains v3; M4 × 8 assembly screws are unchanged.

Install the two M8 bolts into the carrier from the rear, insert the M4 nuts into the clip, then join the parts using M4 × 8 DIN 912 screws. Choose the M8 length to suit the fuse and cable-lug stack.

Default assembly: 27 × 72.8 × 25.2 mm, excluding hardware. The terminal pads are 5.0 mm closer to the DIN rail. Static clearance above the spring arch: 1.0 mm. Centre bridge: 3.1 mm thick. The centre floor is 7.4 mm below the terminal pads; the chamfers leave at least 6.1 mm below the pads over the supplied 29.2 × 19 mm fuse footprint. Confirm the fuse underside clears it on the first print.

Print prototype: 0.2 mm layers, 6 walls, Arachne. This is an untested mechanical prototype, with no established electrical current, thermal or insulation rating. Cable lugs and fuse terminals must meet metal to metal; the printed parts provide mechanical support.
