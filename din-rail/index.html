DIN rail distribution stud

Four size settings. Preview and STL export for printing on its side.

35 mm DIN rail · M6 / M8 / M10

A common-potential connection point for cable lugs, for example in Victron Energy systems. The bolt holds the stacked cable lugs, secured with a nut, to join incoming and outgoing cables for power distribution. The printed holder mounts the stud on a 35 mm DIN rail.

The through-hole and head recess adapt to the selected bolt.
M6 and M8: 1 or 2 modules.
mm
Default stop spacing: 35.75 mm at 0 allowance. Positive values widen the gap; negative values narrow it. Each end moves by half the adjustment.
mm
Both grooves = sheet thickness + 0.20 mm clearance.
Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom

The STL uses millimetres and is oriented for printing on its side. Defaults: M8, 2 modules (36 mm), 0 mm allowance, 1 mm sheet thickness. Default stop spacing: 35.75 mm. Default sheet grooves: 1.20 mm. Changing the holder width also changes the spring stiffness.

Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, Arachne, 6 walls and no supports. Modified variants are dimensional prototypes; their fit and strength need to be checked with a physical print.

Dimensions and model credits

This model uses an 18 mm module. The head recess adds 0.35 mm to the width across flats; the through-hole adds 0.6 mm to the bolt diameter. Recess depths: M6 5.5 mm, M8 6.8 mm, M10 8 mm.

Bolt dimensions: Böllhoff DIN 933. M10 head sizes: Würth, DIN and ISO. 18 mm module: Schneider Electric.

The spring clip mechanism was inspired by Catatat / PlasticXYZ and kc2zgu / marauder156 (CC BY 4.0). The entire configurator and STL export run locally without an internet connection.
