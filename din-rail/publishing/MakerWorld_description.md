# DIN Rail Distribution Stud for Cable Lugs | M6 / M8 / M10

A **distribution stud for joining cable lugs at a common electrical potential**, for example in **Victron Energy systems**. Incoming and outgoing cable lugs are stacked on the bolt and secured with a nut, providing a shared connection point for power distribution. The printed holder mounts the metal stud to a DIN rail.

A one-piece holder for mounting a hex-head bolt on a **35 mm DIN rail**, with the threaded shank pointing outwards. The bolt head inserts from the rear into a recessed hex pocket. One hook stays fixed; the opposite side clips onto the rail using a U-shaped spring and releases through a screwdriver notch.

## Choose a ready-made size or customize your own

Eight STL variants are included:

| Bolt | Holder widths |
|---|---|
| M6, 10 mm head | 18 or 36 mm |
| M8, 13 mm head | 18 or 36 mm |
| M10 ISO, 16 mm head | 27 or 36 mm |
| M10 DIN, 17 mm head | 27 or 36 mm |

One module is 18 mm. M10 uses 1.5 or 2 modules to leave room around its larger head.

The downloadable package includes an **offline HTML configurator** with a 3D preview and STL export. Extract it and open the HTML file in your browser. No account, installation or internet connection is needed. An **OpenSCAD Customizer model** and **STEP files** are included too.

The four inputs are bolt size, holder width, DIN rail width allowance and rail sheet thickness.

## Default dimensions

**M8 · 36 mm wide · 35.75 mm between rail stops · 1.00 mm rail sheet · 1.20 mm flange grooves.**

Rail width allowance ranges from **−0.50 to +0.50 mm**, applied to the 35.75 mm stop spacing and shared equally between the two ends. Sheet thickness ranges from **0.80 to 2.00 mm**. Both grooves include 0.20 mm clearance; the gap between the body support and spring latch stays at 0.40 mm.

## Printing and assembly

Use the supplied side-print orientation. Suggested starting settings: **0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, Arachne, 6 walls, 60% gyroid infill, supports off**. A 4 mm outer brim can help bed adhesion; clear the spring gap afterwards.

Insert the bolt from the rear, engage the fixed rail hook, then press the spring latch over the other flange. Bolt length, nuts and washers depend on the application. Use the release notch to move the latch away from the rail for removal.

The clip design was refined through physical fit trials in PLA. Different widths and materials change the spring stiffness, so check your selected variant with a print. The included Bambu Studio project is for **M8 / 36 mm, X1 Carbon, 0.4 mm nozzle and Generic PLA**. The modular variants have been digitally checked; this does not claim that every variant has been physically printed.

This is a mechanical holder, with no specified electrical current rating or certified insulation rating.
